Privacy

Last updated 21 October 2026. The short version: we keep what you send us, use it to make your ad and talk to you about it, and we don't sell it.

What the "Not ready for a call?" form collects

  • Your name, your email and up to 3 video links.
  • The time you sent it and the page address, including any tags in the link you clicked (such as utm_source or a reference from our email).

We use your videos only to make your example ad and to talk to you about it.

Where it goes

The form goes into a Google Sheet that ZippyScale controls, and our team gets an email that it arrived. Only our team can open them.

The booking calendar on this page is run by cal.com. It loads once the page has finished loading, so cal.com receives a normal page request from your browser. If you book, cal.com also receives your name, email, your answers, the time you pick and any tags in the link. cal.com handles this under its own privacy policy.

Google and cal.com may store this information on servers outside Canada.

How we use it

To make and send your ad, to prepare for your call if you book one, and to contact you about either by email. You can ask us to stop at any time.

Cookies and tracking

This page sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics: no Google Analytics and no Meta pixel. The cal.com calendar may set its own. If that changes, this page will say so first.

Deleting your information

We keep your information only as long as we need it to make your ad and follow up with you.

Email sandy@zippyscale.com to see, correct or delete what we hold about you. We'll delete it and reply to confirm.
