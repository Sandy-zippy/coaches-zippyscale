Questions coaches ask first

I've been burned by an agency. Why is this different?

We count calls held, not leads or replies. We make the ads, not just manage the budget. The person building them is on your weekly call. And under 25 qualified calls held in 90 days of ads, your setup fee and first 3 monthly payments come back.

I hate making content. Do I have to film?

20 minutes a month, on your phone, from our shot list. No posting, no daily content. Old reels, podcast clips and Zoom recordings work too.

What counts as a qualified call?

Someone who passed your budget and fit questions, booked, and showed up. A no-show doesn't count. You set the questions.

What if you miss 25?

If you ran the full 90 days of ads, your setup fee and first 3 monthly payments come back. You keep the page, the ads and the data. Ad spend goes to Meta and isn't part of our fee.

How fast does it start?

Your page and first ads go live by day 14. The 90 days start when ads go live.

How does payment work?

A one-time build, then monthly from day 30. At day 30 you see your cost per call and choose to continue or stop. Stop then and the build isn't refunded; the 90-day guarantee covers coaches who run the full 90 days. We go through the numbers on the call.

Do you work with coaches in Dubai?

Yes. In Dubai, bookings run over WhatsApp, with fast replies and WhatsApp reminders.
