Questions coaches ask first

I've been burned by an agency. Why is this different?

We count calls held, not leads. We make the ads, not just run the budget. The person building them is on your weekly call. And the 25-call guarantee puts our fee on it.

I hate making content. Do I have to film?

20 minutes a month on your phone, from our shot list. No daily posting. Old reels and Zoom clips work too.

What counts as a qualified call?

Someone who passed your budget and fit questions, booked, and showed up. A no-show doesn't count. You set the questions.

How does payment work?

A one-time build, then monthly from day 30. At day 30 you see your cost per call and choose to continue or stop; stop then and the build isn't refunded. Numbers on the call.

Do you work with coaches in Dubai?

Yes. Bookings and reminders run over WhatsApp there.
