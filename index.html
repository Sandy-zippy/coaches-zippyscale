Questions coaches ask first

I've been burned by an agency. Why is this different?

We count calls held, not leads or replies. We make the ads, not just manage the budget. The person building them is on your weekly call. And if we miss 25 qualified calls in 90 days of ads, you get every dollar of our fee back.

I hate making content. Do I have to film?

20 minutes a month, on your phone, from our shot list. No posting, no daily content. Old reels, podcast clips and Zoom recordings work too.

What counts as a qualified call?

Someone who answered your budget and fit questions, booked, and showed up. A no-show doesn't count. You set the questions.

What if you miss 25?

If you ran the full 90 days of ads, every dollar of our fee comes back. You keep the page, the ads and the data. Ad spend goes to Meta and isn't part of our fee.

How fast does it start?

Your page and first ads go live by day 14. The 90 days start when ads go live.

A one-time build, then monthly from day 30. At day 30 you see the scoreboard and decide whether to continue. We go through the numbers on the call.
